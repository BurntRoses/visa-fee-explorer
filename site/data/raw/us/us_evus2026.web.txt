L23:     SUMMARY: The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is announcing Fiscal
L24:     Year (FY) 2026 inflationary adjustments to certain immigration-related
L25:     fees required by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (HR-1). HR-1 requires
L26:     that DHS annually adjust certain immigration-related fees for
L27:     inflation. This notice sets the inflation-adjusted FY 2026 fee amounts
L28:     for the following immigration fees required by HR-1: the fee for
L29:     enrollment in the Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS), the Electronic
L30:     System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) fee, and the fee for an alien
L31:     paroled into the United States. In accordance with HR-1, the existing
L32:     fee for Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record applications will not change
L33:     for FY 2026.
L34: 
L35:     DATES:
L36:         HR-1 Parole Fee: U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), U.S.
L37:     Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), and U.S. Immigration and
L38:     Customs Enforcement (ICE) will begin assessing the FY 2026 amount for
L39:     the HR-1 parole fee described in this notice on January 1, 2026.
L40:         HR-1 I-94, ESTA, and EVUS Fees: CBP will begin assessing the FY
L41:     2026 amounts for the HR-1 I-94, ESTA, and EVUS fees described in this
L42:     notice on January 1, 2026.

L65:     I. Background and Legal Authority
L66: 
L67:         On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law the One Big
L68:     Beautiful Bill Act, Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (HR-1). HR-1 was a
L69:     comprehensive legislative package that changed many laws and added new
L70:     laws that touch many areas of the United States Government. Among those
L71:     changes, the law established new immigration fees and increased
L72:     existing fees for certain immigration-related actions. The statute
L73:     established minimum fees for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 and required annual
L74:     adjustments to the fees in subsequent fiscal years based on the
L75:     Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U).\1\
L76:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L77: 
L78:         \1\ See Public Law 119-21 secs. 100004(d), 100008(b)(2),
L79:     100014(3), 100015(b)(2), codified as 8 U.S.C. 1804(d), 8 U.S.C.
L80:     1807(b)(2), 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(iv), and 8 U.S.C. 1813(b)(2).
L81:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L82: 
L83:         Among other immigration fees specified in HR-1, the Secretary of
L84:     Homeland Security must require the payment of a fee by any alien who is
L85:     paroled into the United States, unless an exception in Public Law 119-
L86:     21 section 100004(b) applies (the HR-1 parole fee). See Public Law 119-
L87:     21 sec. 100004. Several Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
L88:     components, including U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE),
L89:     U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), and U.S. Customs and
L90:     Border Protection (CBP), have responsibilities for assessing and
L91:     collecting the HR-1 parole fee. DHS published a joint notice in the
L92:     Federal Register on October 16, 2025, announcing the implementation of
L93:     the HR-1 parole fee and the FY 2025 rate for the HR-1 parole fee.\2\

L102:     and for enrolling in the Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS). See

L119:     II. FY 2026 Inflation Adjustments to Certain Immigration Fees Required
L120:     by HR-1
L121: 
L122:         In accordance with HR-1, most immigration-related fees required by
L123:     HR-1 are subject to annual inflation adjustments. This notice states
L124:     the FY 2026 rates for the HR-1 parole, I-94, ESTA, and EVUS fees
L125:     (collectively, the HR-1 fees).
L126:         Generally, HR-1 requires the multiplication of an HR-1 fee or a
L127:     portion of an HR-1 fee from the prior FY by the percentage, if any, by
L128:     which the CPI-U for the month of July preceding the date on which the
L129:     adjustment takes effect exceeds the CPI-U for the July of the preceding
L130:     calendar year (the inflation adjustment). In July 2024, the CPI-U was
L131:     314.540, and in July 2025, the CPI-U was 323.048. Therefore, between
L132:     July 2024 and July 2025, the CPI-U increased by 2.70 percent.\5\ To
L133:     determine the total HR-1 fee for each FY, the inflation adjustment is
L134:     then added to the HR-1 fee or portion of the HR-1 fee used in the
L135:     calculation of the inflation adjustment, pursuant to the statutorily
L136:     prescribed formula. The specifics of the statutory inflation adjustment
L137:     formulas differ for each HR-1 fee. The following table provides a
L138:     summary of the inflation-adjusted FY 2026 HR-1 fees described in this
L139:     notice

L167:     A. DHS HR-1 Parole Fee
L168:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L169: 
L170:         \6\ Section II.B. explains the inflation adjustment formula for
L171:     the HR-1 I-94 fee and details why, upon applying the inflation
L172:     adjustment formula and rounding down to the nearest dollar, the fee
L173:     amount will not increase in FY 2026.
L174:         \7\ This $30 total includes the $6 land border fee. Payment of
L175:     the $6 land border fee is required pursuant to 8 CFR 103.7(d)(4),
L176:     235.1(h)(1), and 286.9(b)(1), and is not a fee that is required by
L177:     HR-1.
L178:         \8\ Section II.C. explains the inflation adjustment formula
L179:     found in Public Law 119-21 sec. 100014(3) (8 U.S.C.
L180:     1187(h)(3)(B)(iv)) and the resulting FY 2026 ESTA fee calculations
L181:     for CBP processing of ESTA applications and travel authorizations.
L182:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L183: 
L184:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L185:     the payment of a fee by any alien who is paroled into the United
L186:     States, unless an exception in Public Law 119-21 section 100004(b)
L187:     applies. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004 (8 U.S.C. 1804).
L188:     Specifically, this fee is required each time an alien is granted parole
L189:     under sec. 212(d)(5)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), 8
L190:     U.S.C. 1182(d)(5)(A), including initial parole from outside the United
L191:     States, parole in place, re-parole, or parole from DHS custody. See 90
L192:     FR 48317, 48318.
L193:         During FY 2026 and each subsequent FY, DHS is required to adjust
L194:     the HR-1 parole fee for inflation. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L195:     100004(d). This notice announces the FY 2026 inflation-adjusted HR-1
L196:     parole fee for all DHS components, including ICE, USCIS, and CBP.
L197:         Pursuant to HR-1, DHS is required to adjust the HR-1 parole fee by
L198:     adding an inflation adjustment amount, rounded to the next lowest
L199:     multiple of $10, to the amount of the fee for the most recently
L200:     concluded fiscal year. Id. For FY 2025, HR-1 set an initial minimum fee
L201:     amount of $1,000. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004(c). The FY 2026
L202:     inflation adjustment amount is $27.05 rounded to the next lowest
L203:     multiple of $10, which is $20.
L204:         Accordingly, the inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole fee for FY 2026
L205:     will be $1,020 ($1,000 + $20). The HR-1 parole fee will not be assessed
L206:     if the alien establishes, to the satisfaction of DHS, on an individual,
L207:     case-by-case basis, that the circumstances of the alien's parole align
L208:     with at least 1 of the 10 exceptions found in HR-1. See Public Law 119-
L209:     21 sec. 100004(b). The operative event that triggers the statutory
L210:     obligation to pay the HR-1 parole fee is the actual grant and
L211:     effectuation of parole at or into the United States--not the filing of
L212:     an application or request. Thus, unless statutorily excepted, the FY
L213:     2026 inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole fee will be collected from any
L214:     alien who is granted parole on or after January 1, 2026, regardless of
L215:     when the underlying application or request was submitted.
L216:         The $1,020 HR-1 parole fee is required in addition to any other fee
L217:     authorized by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004(a).
L218:     B. CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record
L219: 
L220:         CBP issues an electronic CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record to
L221:     all arriving aliens who are legally required to submit that form
L222:     (unless otherwise exempted). See parts 1.4 and 235.1(h) of title 8 of
L223:     the Code of Federal Regulations (8 CFR 1.4, 235.1(h)). CBP Form I-94
L224:     serves as evidence of the terms of the alien's admission or parole and
L225:     is generally issued at the time the alien is admitted or paroled at a
L226:     U.S. port of entry. See 8 CFR 235.1(h). Aliens arriving at a land
L227:     border port of entry who are legally required to submit a CBP Form I-94
L228:     must apply for that form.
L229:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L230:     the payment of a fee for any alien who submits an application for a
L231:     Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record (the HR-1 I-94 fee). See Public Law
L232:     119-21 sec. 100008 (8 U.S.C. 1807). For FY 2025, the statute set an
L233:     initial minimum fee amount of $24. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L234:     100008(b)(1).
L235:         During FY 2026 and each subsequent FY, CBP is required to adjust
L236:     the HR-1 I-94 fee for inflation. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L237:     100008(b)(2). The statutory formula requires adding an inflation
L238:     adjustment amount, rounded down to the nearest dollar, to the amount of
L239:     the fee required under Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(b) for the most
L240:     recently concluded fiscal year. Id. For FY 2026, the inflation
L241:     adjustment required by Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(b)(2)(B) is $0.65
L242:     rounded down to the nearest dollar, which is $0. Thus, the HR-1 I-94
L243:     fee for FY 2026 will remain at $24 ($24 + $0).
L244:         The HR-1 I-94 fee is required in addition to any other fee
L245:     authorized by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(a). Currently, CBP
L246:     imposes a $6 fee for aliens who are legally required to be issued, or
L247:     request to be issued, CBP Form I-94 and who intend to arrive at a land
L248:     border port of entry. See 8 CFR 103.7(d)(4), 235.1(h)(1), and
L249:     286.9(b)(1). Accordingly, the total fee to apply for a CBP Form I-94 at
L250:     a land border port of entry for FY 2026 will continue to be $30,
L251:     consisting of the $6 land border fee and the $24 HR-1 fee. CBP will not
L252:     assess a fee for aliens arriving at an air or sea port of entry because
L253:     such aliens are not required to submit an application for a CBP Form I-
L254:     94.

L256:     C. Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA)
L257: 
L258:         ESTA is the online system through which aliens intending to enter
L259:     the United States under the Visa Waiver Program (VWP) must obtain an
L260:     electronic travel authorization in advance of travel to the United
L261:     States.\9\ Each alien intending to travel by air, sea, or land to the
L262:     United States under the VWP must receive a travel authorization via
L263:     ESTA prior to travel. See INA sec. 217 (8 U.S.C. 1187) and 8 CFR part
L264:     217. Prior to the enactment of HR-1, CBP required a fee of $21 for each
L265: 
L266:     [[Page 52087]]
L267: 
L268:     ESTA authorization. Section 100014 of Public Law 119-21 amended section
L269:     217(h)(3)(B) of the INA (8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)) to increase the fee
L270:     for ESTA authorizations to $40 in FY 2025.
L271:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L272: 
L273:         \9\ For additional information on the Visa Waiver Program, see 8
L274:     U.S.C. 1187 and 8 CFR part 217.
L275:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L276: 
L277:         The FY 2025 ESTA authorization fee of $40, as required by HR-1, is
L278:     the sum of three fees, including: $17 per travel authorization as
L279:     provided for in 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(I), $10 as provided for in 8
L280:     U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) to ensure recovery of the full costs of
L281:     providing and administering the ESTA System, and $13 per travel
L282:     authorization as provided for in 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(III). The 8
L283:     U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee is required for all
L284:     applications, regardless of authorization or denial; thus, in the event
L285:     the ESTA application is denied, HR-1 set a $10 fee for FY 2025. See 8
L286:     U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B).
L287:         During FY 2026 and each subsequent FY, CBP is required to adjust
L288:     the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee for inflation. See
L289:     8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(iv). Under HR-1, the adjusted 8 U.S.C.
L290:     1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee is calculated by adding the
L291:     amount required under 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) for the previous FY
L292:     to the inflation adjustment amount. See 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(iv). For
L293:     FY 2025, the amount required under 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) was
L294:     $10. The inflation adjustment for FY 2026 is $0.27. HR-1 does not
L295:     direct CBP to round the HR-1 ESTA inflation adjustment. Thus, for FY
L296:     2026, the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee will be
L297:     $10.27 ($10 + $0.27).
L298:         As noted previously, the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost
L299:     recovery fee is one of three fees that, when combined, comprise the
L300:     total ESTA authorization fee for each FY. This notice does not alter
L301:     the $17 or $13 fees required under 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(I) and 8
L302:     U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(III), respectively. Thus, as required by HR-1,
L303:     the inflation-adjusted FY 2026 ESTA fee will be $40.27 ($17 + $10.27 +
L304:     $13) per travel authorization. In the event the ESTA application is
L305:     denied, the FY 2026 fee is $10.27 to ensure recovery of the costs of
L306:     providing and administering the ESTA System.
L307:     D. Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS)
L308: 
L309:         EVUS is an online system currently used by nationals of the
L310:     People's Republic of China (PRC) holding a 10-year B-1, B-2, or B-1/B-2
L311:     (visitor) visa to provide required information to DHS prior to travel
L312:     to the United States. See 8 CFR part 215, subpart B.\10\ PRC nationals
L313:     with an approved U.S.-issued visa of a designated category must enroll
L314:     in EVUS and provide or update personal and travel information to
L315:     receive a determination of travel eligibility. See 8 CFR 215.24.
L316:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L317: 
L318:         \10\ In a Federal Register notice published on October 20, 2016
L319:     (81 FR 72600), DHS identified the PRC as an EVUS country and
L320:     designated B-1, B-2, and B-1/B-2 visas issued without restriction
L321:     for the maximum validity period and contained in a passport issued
L322:     by the PRC as designated visa categories for purposes of EVUS.
L323:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L324: 
L325:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L326:     the payment of a fee by any alien enrolling in EVUS. Public Law 119-21
L327:     sec. 100015 (8 U.S.C. 1813). The alien must pay the fee at the time of
L328:     enrollment. Id. For FY 2025, the statute set a minimum fee amount of
L329:     $30. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(b)(1).
L330:         During FY 2026 and each subsequent FY, CBP is required to adjust
L331:     the HR-1 EVUS fee for inflation. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L332:     100015(b)(2). The statutory formula requires adding an inflation
L333:     adjustment amount, rounded down to the next lowest multiple of $0.25,
L334:     to the amount of the fee required under Public Law 119-21 sec.
L335:     100015(b) for the most recently concluded fiscal year. Id. For FY 2026,
L336:     the inflation adjustment under Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(b)(2)(B)
L337:     is $0.81 rounded down to the next lowest multiple of $0.25, which is
L338:     $0.75. Thus, the inflation-adjusted HR-1 EVUS fee for FY 2026 is $30.75
L339:     ($30 + $0.75).
L340:         This $30.75 HR-1 EVUS fee is required in addition to any other fee
L341:     applicable by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(a).
