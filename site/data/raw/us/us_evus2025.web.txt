L22:     SUMMARY: This notice advises the public that, pursuant to HR-1, U.S.
L23:     Customs and Border Protection (CBP) will require aliens to pay a new
L24:     fee to apply for a CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record, and to
L25:     enroll in the Electronic Visa Update System. Additionally, pursuant to
L26:     HR-1, the Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA) fee for
L27:     authorizations has increased. This notice advises the public of the HR-
L28:     1 applicable fees for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025.
L29: 
L30:     DATES: CBP will begin assessing the new HR-1 fees described in this
L31:     notice on September 30, 2025.

L40:     I. Background and Legal Authority
L41: 
L42:         On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law the One Big
L43:     Beautiful Bill Act, Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (``HR-1''). HR-1
L44:     was a comprehensive legislative package that changed many laws and
L45:     added new laws that touch many areas of the United States
L46:     Government.\1\ Among those changes, the law established new immigration
L47:     enforcement fees and increased existing fees for certain immigration-
L48:     related actions. The statute establishes minimum fees for Fiscal Year
L49:     (FY) 2025 and requires annual increases to the fees based on the
L50:     Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers.\2\
L51:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L52: 
L53:         \1\ See HR-1, Title X, Subtitle A, Part I, sections 100001
L54:     through 100018.
L55:         \2\ See Public Law 119-21 secs. 100008(b)(2), 100014(3),
L56:     100015(b)(2).
L57:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L58: 
L59:         This notice states the FY 2025 rates for three fees specified in
L60:     HR-1 that are generally administered by U.S. Customs and Border
L61:     Protection (CBP).\3\ Specifically, this notice states the fees
L62:     applicable for the application of CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure
L63:     Record, for travel authorizations via the Electronic System for Travel
L64:     Authorization (ESTA), and for enrollment in the Electronic Visa Update
L65:     System (EVUS). See Public Law 119-21 secs. 100008, 100014, 100015. The
L66:     funds collected from these fees will be distributed to the appropriate
L67:     agency or the U.S. Treasury as mandated by statute.\4\ The following
L68:     table provides a summary of the FY 2025 fees described in this notice
L69:     and required by HR-1.

L93:     II. FY 2025 Immigration Fees Required by HR-1
L94:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L95: 
L96:         \5\ As described above in section II.B, the total fee for aliens
L97:     who apply for a CBP Form I-94 (i.e., aliens arriving at a land
L98:     border port of entry) will be $30, consisting of the $6 fee required
L99:     by 8 CFR 103.7(d)(4), 235.1(h)(1) and 286.9(b)(1), and the $24 fee
L100:     required by HR-1.
L101:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L102: 
L103:     A. CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record
L104: 
L105:         CBP issues an electronic CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record to
L106:     all arriving aliens who are legally required to submit that form
L107:     (unless otherwise exempted). See 8 CFR 1.4, 235.1(h). CBP Form I-94
L108:     serves as evidence of the terms of the alien's admission or parole and
L109:     is generally issued at the time the alien is admitted or paroled at a
L110:     U.S. port of entry. See 8 CFR 235.1(h). Aliens arriving at a land
L111:     border port of entry must apply for a CBP Form I-94.
L112:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L113:     the payment of a fee for any alien who submits an application for a
L114:     Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008.
L115:     For FY 2025, the statute sets a minimum fee of $24. See Public Law 119-
L116:     21 sec. 100008(b)(1). Accordingly, for FY 2025, the minimum HR-1 fee to
L117:     apply for a CBP Form I-94 is $24.
L118:         The $24 fee is in addition to any other fee applicable by law. See
L119:     Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(a). Currently, CBP imposes a $6 fee for
L120:     aliens who are required to be issued, or request to be issued, CBP Form
L121:     I-94 and who intend to arrive at a land border port of entry. See 8 CFR
L122:     103.7(d)(4), 235.1(h)(1) and 286.9(b)(1). Accordingly, the total fee to
L123:     apply for a CBP Form I-94 at a land border port of entry for FY 2025 is
L124:     $30, consisting of the $6 land border fee and the HR-1 $24 fee. CBP
L125:     will not assess a fee for aliens arriving at an air or sea port of
L126:     entry because such aliens are not required to submit an application for
L127:     a CBP Form I-94.
L128: 
L129:     B. Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA)
L130: 
L131:         ESTA is the online system through which aliens intending to enter
L132:     the United States under the Visa Waiver Program (VWP) must obtain an
L133:     electronic travel authorization in advance of travel to the United
L134:     States.\6\ Each alien intending to travel by air, sea, or land to the
L135:     United States under the VWP must receive a travel authorization via
L136:     ESTA prior to travel. See sec. 217 of the Immigration and Nationality
L137:     Act (INA) (8 U.S.C. 1187) and 8 CFR part 217. Prior to the enactment of
L138:     HR-1, CBP required a fee of $21 for an ESTA.
L139:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L140:         \6\ For additional information on the Visa Waiver Program, see 8
L141:     U.S.C. 1187 and 8 CFR part 217.
L142: 
L143:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L144: 
L145:     [[Page 42026]]
L146: 
L147:         Section 100014 of Public Law 119-21 amended section 217(h)(3)(B) of
L148:     the INA (8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)) to increase the fee for the use of
L149:     ESTA to $40. The minimum ESTA fee for FY 2025 is now $40.\7\
L150:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L151: 
L152:         \7\ The ESTA fee is now the sum of three amounts: (1) $17 per
L153:     travel authorization fee, pre-existing in (B)(i)(I); (2) an amount
L154:     not less than $10, set now in (B)(i)(II); and (3) the new $13 per
L155:     travel authorization fee in (B)(i)(III), for a total minimum of $40.
L156:     See INA section 217(h)(3)(B), as amended by sec. 100014, Public Law
L157:     119-21.
L158:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

L160:     C. Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS)
L161: 
L162:         EVUS is an online system currently used by nationals of the
L163:     People's Republic of China (PRC) holding a 10-year B1/B2, B1 or B2
L164:     (visitor) visa to provide required information to the Department of
L165:     Homeland Security (DHS) prior to travel to the United States. See 8 CFR
L166:     part 215, subpart B.\8\ PRC nationals with an approved U.S.-issued visa
L167:     of a designated category must enroll in EVUS and provide or update
L168:     personal and travel information to receive a determination of travel
L169:     eligibility. See 8 CFR 214.24.
L170:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L171: 
L172:         \8\ In a Federal Register notice published on Oct. 20, 2016 (81
L173:     FR 72600), DHS identified the PRC as an EVUS country and designated
L174:     B-1, B-2, and B-1/B-2 visas issued without restriction for the
L175:     maximum validity period and contained in a passport issued by the
L176:     PRC as designated visa categories for purposes of EVUS.
L177:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L178: 
L179:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L180:     the payment of a fee by any alien enrolling in EVUS. Public Law 119-21
L181:     sec. 100015. The alien must pay the fee at the time of enrollment. Id.
L182:     For FY 2025, the statute sets a minimum fee of $30. See Public Law 119-
L183:     21 sec. 100015(b)(1). This $30 fee is in addition to any other fee
L184:     applicable by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(a).
