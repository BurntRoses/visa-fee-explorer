L23:     SUMMARY: The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is announcing Fiscal
L24:     Year (FY) 2027 inflation adjustments to certain immigration-related
L25:     fees required by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (HR-1). HR-1 requires
L26:     that DHS annually adjust certain immigration-related fees for
L27:     inflation. This notice sets the inflation-adjusted FY 2027 fee amounts
L28:     for the following immigration fees required by HR-1: the fee for parole
L29:     of an alien into the United States, the Electronic System for Travel
L30:     Authorization (ESTA) fees, and the fee for enrollment in the Electronic
L31:     Visa Update System (EVUS). In accordance with the inflation adjustment
L32:     rounding requirement in HR-1, the existing fee for Form I-94 Arrival/
L33:     Departure Record applications will not change for FY 2027.
L34: 
L35:     DATES: HR-1 Parole Fee: U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), U.S.
L36:     Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), and U.S. Immigration and
L37:     Customs Enforcement (ICE) will begin assessing the FY 2027 amount for
L38:     the HR-1 parole fee described in this notice on October 16, 2026.
L39:         HR-1 I-94, ESTA, and EVUS Fees: CBP will begin assessing the FY
L40:     2027 amounts for the HR-1 I-94, ESTA, and EVUS fees described in this
L41:     notice on October 16, 2026.

L64:     I. Background and Legal Authority
L65: 
L66:         On July 4, 2025, the President signed into law the One Big
L67:     Beautiful Bill Act,
L68: 
L69:     [[Page 62535]]
L70: 
L71:     Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (HR-1). HR-1 was a comprehensive
L72:     legislative package that changed many laws and added new laws that
L73:     touch many areas of the United States Government. Among those changes,
L74:     the law established new immigration fees and increased existing fees
L75:     for certain immigration-related actions. The statute established
L76:     minimum fees for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 and required annual adjustments
L77:     to the fees in subsequent fiscal years based on the Consumer Price
L78:     Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U).\1\

L128:     II. FY 2027 Inflation Adjustments to Certain Immigration Fees Required
L129:     by HR-1
L130: 
L131:         In accordance with HR-1, most immigration-related fees required by
L132:     HR-1 are subject to annual inflation adjustments. This notice provides
L133:     the FY 2027 rates for the HR-1 parole, I-94, ESTA, and EVUS fees
L134:     (collectively, the HR-1 fees).
L135:         Generally, HR-1 requires the multiplication of an HR-1 fee or a
L136:     portion of an HR-1 fee from the prior fiscal year by the percentage, if
L137:     any, by which the CPI-U for the month of July preceding the date on
L138:     which the adjustment takes effect exceeds the CPI-U for the July of the
L139:     preceding calendar year (the inflation adjustment). In July 2025, the
L140:     CPI-U was 323.048, and in July 2026, the CPI-U was 333.918. Therefore,
L141:     between July 2025 and July 2026, the CPI-U increased by 3.36
L142:     percent.\3\ To determine the total HR-1 fee for each fiscal year, the
L143:     inflation adjustment is added to the HR-1 fee or portion of the HR-1
L144:     fee used in the calculation of the inflation adjustment, pursuant to
L145:     the statutorily prescribed formula. The specifics of the statutory
L146:     inflation adjustment formulas differ for each HR-1 fee. The following
L147:     table provides a summary of the inflation-adjusted FY 2027 HR-1 fees
L148:     described in this notice, and the following subsections further detail
L149:     the HR-1 inflation adjustment requirements.
L150:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L151:

L153:     August 12, 2026, 【3†https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_08122026.htm†www.bls.gov】 (last visited Aug. 13, 2026).
L154:         \4\ Section II.B. explains the inflation adjustment formula for
L155:     the HR-1 I-94 fee and details that upon applying the inflation
L156:     adjustment formula and rounding down to the nearest dollar, the fee
L157:     amount will not increase in FY 2027.

L161:     HR-1. Therefore, the inflation adjustment is not calculated based on
L162:     the total $30 fee, and instead, only the $24 portion of the Form I-
L163:     94 fee, required by HR-1, is adjusted for inflation.

L187:     A. DHS HR-1 Parole Fee
L188: 
L189:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L190:     the payment of a fee by any alien who is paroled into the United
L191:     States, unless an exception in Public Law 119-21 section 100004(b)
L192:     applies. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004 (8 U.S.C. 1804).
L193:     Specifically, this fee is required each time an alien is granted parole
L194:     under sec. 212(d)(5)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA), 8
L195:     U.S.C. 1182(d)(5)(A), including initial parole from outside the United
L196:     States, parole in place, re-parole, or parole from DHS custody. See 90
L197:     FR 48317, 48318.
L198:         DHS is required to adjust the HR-1 parole fee for inflation in each
L199:     fiscal year. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004(d). This notice
L200:     announces the FY 2027 inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole fee for all DHS
L201:     components, including ICE, USCIS, and CBP.
L202:         Pursuant to HR-1, DHS is required to adjust the HR-1 parole fee by
L203:     adding an inflation adjustment amount, rounded to the next lowest
L204:     multiple of $10, to the amount of the fee for the most recently
L205:     concluded fiscal year. Id. The FY 2026 inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole
L206:     fee is $1,020. See 90 FR 52085 (Nov. 19, 2025). The FY 2027 inflation
L207:     adjustment amount is $34.32 rounded to the next lowest multiple of $10,
L208:     which is $30.
L209:         Accordingly, the inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole fee for FY 2027
L210:     will be $1,050 ($1,020 + $30). The HR-1 parole fee will not be assessed
L211:     if the alien establishes, to the satisfaction of DHS, on an individual,
L212:     case-by-case basis, that the circumstances of the alien's parole align
L213:     with at least 1 of the 10 exceptions found in HR-1. See Public Law 119-
L214:     21 sec. 100004(b). The operative event that triggers the statutory
L215:     obligation to pay the HR-1 parole fee is the actual grant and
L216:     effectuation of parole into the United States--not the filing of an
L217:     application or request. Thus, unless statutorily excepted, the FY 2027
L218:     inflation-adjusted HR-1 parole fee will be collected from any alien who
L219:     is granted parole on or after October 16, 2026, regardless of when the
L220:     underlying application or request was submitted.
L221:         The $1,050 HR-1 parole fee is required in addition to any other fee
L222:     authorized by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100004(a).
L223: 
L224:     B. CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record
L225: 
L226:         CBP issues an electronic CBP Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record to
L227:     all arriving aliens who are legally required to submit that form
L228:     (unless otherwise exempted). See parts 1.4 and 235.1(h) of title 8 of
L229:     the Code of Federal Regulations (8 CFR 1.4, 235.1(h)). CBP Form I-94
L230:     serves as evidence of the terms of the alien's admission or parole and
L231:     is generally issued at the time the alien is admitted or paroled at a
L232:     U.S. port of entry. See 8 CFR 235.1(h). Aliens arriving at a land
L233:     border port of entry who are legally required to submit a CBP Form I-94
L234:     must apply for that form.
L235:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L236:     the payment of a fee for any alien who submits an application for a
L237:     Form I-94 Arrival/Departure Record (the HR-1 I-94 fee). See Public Law
L238:     119-21 sec. 100008 (8 U.S.C. 1807). CBP is required to adjust the HR-1
L239:     I-94 fee for inflation in each fiscal year. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L240:     100008(b)(2). The statutory formula requires adding an inflation
L241:     adjustment amount, rounded down to the nearest dollar, to the amount of
L242:     the fee required under Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(b) for the most
L243:     recently concluded fiscal year. Id. The FY 2026 HR-1 I-94 fee is $24.
L244:     See 90 FR 52085, 52086. For FY 2027, the inflation adjustment required
L245:     by Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(b)(2)(B) is $0.81 rounded down to the
L246:     nearest dollar, which is $0. Thus, the HR-1 I-94 fee for FY 2026 will
L247:     remain at $24 ($24 + $0).
L248:         The HR-1 I-94 fee is required in addition to any other fee
L249:     authorized by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100008(a). Currently, CBP
L250:     imposes a $6 fee for aliens who are legally required to be issued, or
L251:     request to be issued, CBP Form I-94 and who intend to arrive at a land
L252:     border port of entry. See 8 CFR 103.7(d)(4), 235.1(h)(1), and
L253:     286.9(b)(1). Accordingly, the total fee to apply for a CBP Form I-94 at
L254:     a land border port of entry for FY 2027 will continue to be $30,
L255:     consisting of the $6 land border fee and the $24 HR-1 fee. CBP will not
L256:     assess a fee for aliens arriving at an air or sea port of entry because
L257:     such aliens are not required to submit an application for a CBP Form I-
L258:     94.

L260:     C. Electronic System for Travel Authorization (ESTA)
L261: 
L262:         ESTA is the online system through which aliens intending to enter
L263:     the United States under the Visa Waiver Program (VWP) must obtain an
L264:     electronic travel authorization in advance of travel to the United
L265:     States.\7\ Each alien intending to travel by air, sea, or land to the
L266:     United States under the VWP must receive a travel authorization via
L267:     ESTA prior to travel. See INA sec. 217 (8 U.S.C. 1187) and 8 CFR part
L268:     217. Pursuant to section 217(h)(3)(B) of the INA (8 U.S.C.
L269:     1187(h)(3)(B)), as amended by section 100014 of HR-1, two fees are
L270:     required for each ESTA authorization, and one fee is required for each
L271:     use of the ESTA system.
L272:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L273: 
L274:         \7\ For additional information on the Visa Waiver Program, see 8
L275:     U.S.C. 1187 and 8 CFR part 217.
L276:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L277: 
L278:         The FY 2026 ESTA authorization fee of $40.27, as required by HR-1,
L279:     is the sum of three fees, including: $17 per travel authorization as
L280:     provided for in 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(I), $10.27 as provided for in
L281:     8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) to ensure recovery of the full costs of
L282:     providing and administering the ESTA system, and $13 per travel
L283:     authorization as provided for in 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(III). See 90
L284:     FR 52085, 52087. The 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee is
L285:     required for all applications, regardless of authorization or denial;
L286:     thus, in the event the ESTA application is denied, HR-1 set a $10.27
L287:     fee for FY 2026. See 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B).
L288:         CBP is required to adjust the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost
L289:     recovery fee for inflation in each fiscal year. See 8 U.S.C.
L290:     1187(h)(3)(B)(iv). Under HR-1, the adjusted 8 U.S.C.
L291:     1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost recovery fee is calculated by adding the
L292:     amount required under 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) for the previous
L293:     fiscal year to the inflation adjustment amount. See 8 U.S.C.
L294:     1187(h)(3)(B)(iv). For FY 2026, the amount required under 8 U.S.C.
L295:     1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) is $10.27. The inflation adjustment for FY 2027 is
L296:     $0.35. HR-1 does not direct CBP to round the HR-1 ESTA inflation
L297:     adjustment. Thus, for FY 2027, the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost
L298:     recovery fee will be $10.62 ($10.27 + $0.35).
L299:         As noted previously, the 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(II) cost
L300:     recovery fee is one of three fees that, when combined, comprise the
L301:     total ESTA authorization fee for each fiscal year. This notice does not
L302:     alter the $17 or $13 fees required under 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(I)
L303:     and 8 U.S.C. 1187(h)(3)(B)(i)(III), respectively. Thus, as required by
L304:     HR-1, the inflation-adjusted FY 2027 ESTA fee will be $40.62 ($17 +
L305:     $10.62 + $13) per travel authorization. In the event the ESTA
L306:     application is denied, the FY 2027 fee is $10.62 to ensure recovery of
L307:     the costs of providing and administering the ESTA System.

L309:     D. Electronic Visa Update System (EVUS)
L310:         EVUS is an online system currently used by nationals of the
L311:     People's Republic of China (PRC) holding a 10-year B-1, B-2, or B-1/B-2
L312:     (visitor) visa to provide required information to DHS prior to travel
L313:     to the United States. See
L314: 
L315:     [[Page 62537]]
L316: 
L317:     8 CFR part 215, subpart B.\8\ PRC nationals with an approved U.S.-
L318:     issued visa of a designated category must enroll in EVUS and provide or
L319:     update personal and travel information to receive a determination of
L320:     travel eligibility. See 8 CFR 215.24.
L321:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L322: 
L323:         \8\ In a Federal Register notice published on October 20, 2016
L324:     (81 FR 72600), DHS identified the PRC as an EVUS country and
L325:     designated B-1, B-2, and B-1/B-2 visas issued without restriction
L326:     for the maximum validity period and contained in a passport issued
L327:     by the PRC as designated visa categories for purposes of EVUS.
L328:     ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
L329: 
L330:         Pursuant to HR-1, the Secretary of Homeland Security must require
L331:     any alien enrolling in EVUS to pay a fee. Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015
L332:     (8 U.S.C. 1813). The alien must pay the fee at the time of enrollment.
L333:     Id. For FY 2025, the statute set a minimum fee amount of $30. See
L334:     Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(b)(1).
L335:         During FY 2026 and each subsequent FY, CBP is required to adjust
L336:     the HR-1 EVUS fee for inflation. See Public Law 119-21 sec.
L337:     100015(b)(2). The statutory formula requires adding an inflation
L338:     adjustment amount, rounded down to the next lowest multiple of $0.25,
L339:     to the amount of the fee required under Public Law 119-21 sec.
L340:     100015(b) for the most recently concluded fiscal year. Id. The
L341:     inflation-adjusted HR-1 EVUS fee for FY 2026 is $30.75. For FY 2027,
L342:     the inflation adjustment under Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(b)(2)(B)
L343:     is $1.03 rounded down to the next lowest multiple of $0.25, which is
L344:     $1.00. Thus, the inflation-adjusted HR-1 EVUS fee for FY 2027 is $31.75
L345:     ($30.75 + $1.00).
L346:         This $31.75 HR-1 EVUS fee is required in addition to any other fee
L347:     applicable by law. See Public Law 119-21 sec. 100015(a).
