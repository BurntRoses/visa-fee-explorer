Pilgrim Experience Program Reports Over 19.5 Million International Pilgrims and Umrah Performers in 2025

03 Aug 2026

The Pilgrim Experience Program (PEP), one of Saudi Vision 2030 realization programs, has issued its 2025 Annual Report under the theme “Honor and Impact,” highlighting the most notable achievements of the Pilgrim Experience ecosystem and the outcomes of its initiatives and projects in continuously developing the services provided to pilgrims, Umrah performers, and visitors. These efforts aim to enhance the quality of their journey and enrich their experience, from the planning stage through to their return to their home countries.

The report stated that the PEP implemented 87 initiatives in 2025 in partnership with more than 60 implementing entities, achieving a 100% compliance rate in initiative implementation. This underscores the Kingdom’s longstanding, deeply rooted commitment to serving pilgrims and contributes to achieving the objectives of Saudi Vision 2030, which aims to elevate the quality of services provided to pilgrims and visitors.

The report indicated that, in 2025, more than 19.5 million pilgrims and Umrah performers from abroad arrived in Saudi Arabia, including 18.03 million Umrah performers and 1.5 million pilgrims. The number of Umrah performers increased by 114% compared with 2022, representing a significant achievement that reflects the ecosystem’s readiness and the efficiency of the services provided.

Despite continued growth in visitor numbers, the indicators of pilgrim experience satisfaction remained high. Hajj pilgrim satisfaction reached 91%, while Umrah performer satisfaction reached 94%, exceeding the Saudi Vision 2030 targets for both indicators. In addition, Al-Rawdah Al-Sharifah welcomed more than 16 million visitors in 2025, while visitor satisfaction increased to 88%, up from 57% in 2022. These results demonstrate the impact of the efforts undertaken to improve services and facilitate the performance of rituals in accordance with the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

As part of efforts to enrich the religious and cultural experience, the report stated that the number of developed and visitor-ready historical sites and enrichment destinations in Makkah and Madinah increased to 87, attracting more than 55 million visits. This milestone enriches the pilgrims' experience and strengthens their connection to Islamic historical heritage.

Regarding enhancing community participation, the report recorded 184,569 male and female volunteers serving pilgrims and visitors in 2025. This reflects the growing culture of volunteerism among the people of the Kingdom and highlights its pivotal role in improving service quality.

The Kingdom also achieved advanced positions in international rankings. Makkah ranked fifth globally in the index of cities receiving the highest numbers of international visitors, while Madinah ranked seventh globally in the Tourism Performance Index.

As part of the continued development of digital services, the report indicated that a total of 15.03 million visas, including Umrah, visit, and other categories were issued, with Umrah visas accounting for 83% of the total. Pakistan, Indonesia, and Egypt were the leading countries in terms of Umrah visa arrivals, reflecting the integration of efforts to facilitate pilgrims' and visitors' journeys and enhance their overall experience.

The report concluded by affirming that these efforts contribute to fulfilling the aspirations of Saudi Vision 2030 and embody the Kingdom’s leading role in serving the Two Holy Mosques and those who visit them.
