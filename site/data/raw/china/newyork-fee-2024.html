Notice on Reducing Visa Fee

2023-12-08 22:15

To facilitate people-people exchanges, the Chinese Embassy and Consulates-General in the US will reduce the visa fees. The reduced fees are as follows:

Note: 1. The visa fees listed above are applicable from December 11, 2023 to December 31, 2024.

    *2. Visa fees for certain non-U.S.Citizens vary from the list above. Please check with the Visa Office for details.

3. For an express service, there is an additional fee of $25 per visa.

Visa applications submitted before December 11, 2023 cannot enjoy the fees reduction.
